# code for <good>
## A Tech for Social Good program

**Organization**: No Food Waste  
**Website**: www.nofoodwaste.org  

---

### Mission
No Food Waste is a non-profit organization dedicated to bridging the gap between food surplus and hunger. Its mission is to recover excess, untouched, and hygienic food from weddings, restaurants, and institutions and redistribute it to people in need. No Food Waste aims to create a world with zero hunger and minimal food waste, aligning its efforts with global sustainability goals of Zero Hunger and responsible consumption.

---

### Context
India faces a dual crisis: millions of people go hungry daily, while over 68 million tons of food is wasted annually. No Food Waste is dedicated to bridging this gap by collecting and distributing surplus food to undernourished communities across various locations in India, including Chennai, Coimbatore, and Hyderabad. The organization operates with a full-time kitchen, a team of volunteers, and a few vehicles to manage food distribution.

Despite their efforts, the manual process of handling food donation requests through helpline numbers during peak days often leads to resource constraints and rejected requests. Data management is currently conducted manually using spreadsheets and pictures shared in WhatsApp groups, posing significant challenges. While vehicles equipped with GPS trackers are utilized for routine food collection, coordinating their availability and effectively managing smaller quantities of excess food continues to be a challenging task.

---

### Strategy

#### Current Stage
- Excess food requests are submitted through the NGO's helpline numbers, and admins manually enter the details into spreadsheets, deciding to accept or reject requests based on resource availability.
- Requests involving smaller quantities are often rejected due to resource constraints. The NGO also collaborates with various corporates and organizations to take excess food requests.
- All necessary data is manually entered into a spreadsheet, and volunteers and vehicle owners are informed based on their availability.
- Volunteers, trained in food safety measures, collect food from requesting parties and donate it while adhering to all food safety protocols, documenting each step with photographs.
- All communications with volunteers are conducted via WhatsApp or phone calls, including the sharing of collected data and photos.
- Admins manually organize the data collected in spreadsheets, integrating it with photos shared by volunteers. This process facilitates detailed analysis and the generation of statistics, which are essential for presenting insights to stakeholders, donors, and management.

#### Next Stage
- No Food Waste is seeking a comprehensive, technology-driven platform to optimize operations and facilitate real-time management of food donation requests, volunteer availability, and logistics.
- The platform aims to minimize resource constraints and reduce rejected requests by optimizing resource utilization, particularly for smaller quantities of excess food, and automating data collection and management for enhanced analysis.
- An integrated system that ensures efficient coordination with volunteers and response to food collection inquiries, thereby streamlining interactions and decision-making.
- They are keen on leveraging data and connectivity to map hunger hotspots, ensuring timely distribution within the golden hour and amplifying the overall impact of their efforts.
- No Food Waste is focused on building a network of volunteers across various locations to support their regular food collection needs. They plan to engage volunteers consistently and provide them with effective training and certification.

---

### Stakeholder Universe

#### Institutions & Individuals raising excess food requests to the NGO:
- A platform that lets users raise requests independently, without relying on the NGO's helpline, and provides a mechanism to track their requests.
- If the NGO is unable to fulfill a request, it should have the capability to identify nearby needy families and facilitate direct donations, ensuring compliance with all food safety protocols.

#### Volunteer/Vehicle owners:
- There is a need for a communication system to streamline the volunteer engagement process and data collection during various stages, simplifying manual tasks and enhancing efficiency.
- A capability to track volunteer training progress.

#### Admins/NGO partners:
- An effective solution to streamline the data collection and analysis process and to track volunteer/vehicle availability, enhancing operational efficiency.

---

### Challenge
No Food Waste faces challenge with the food donation request coming in through their helpline numbers, making all their process manual. To address these challenges, No Food Waste seeks a technology-driven platform to facilitate real-time food donation requests, coordinate volunteers and logistics, map hunger hotspots, and ensure timely distribution within golden hour.

---

### Technology Baseline
No Food Waste currently manages operations manually, utilizing helpline numbers to handle excess food requests. Data management is conducted through spreadsheets, while communication with volunteers and NGO partners is facilitated via WhatsApp or phone calls. The organization operates with a dedicated 12-member team in Chennai and employs five vehicles for food distribution. These volunteers are thoroughly trained in food safety protocols to ensure the effective collection and donation of food to those in need.
